Here are a few artists that inspire me


Dyperec

I really like their use of textures and how text is blended perfectly.
They are able to create a consistent visual aesthetic with very experimental and unconventional techniques.


Landscape Design Agency

Landscape is a design agency based in SF. I know some folks who work here and they are very talented.
I particularly like their motion based work more than anything. It's very smooth while also tricking the brain into thinking the work is complex.


Sometimes Always

Sometimes Always is a design studio based in São Paolo.
I particularly like the work they did for Public Records NYC because of how simple and styllistic it is.
They were able to pull off something very minimal, while also maintaining a strong brand voice for Public Records.
I feel like this is one of the hardest things to do as a brand designer.
