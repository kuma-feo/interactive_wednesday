Reflection


In general this project was quite simple. I mostly focused on following the directions and making sure the content was named correctly. Every now and then I would un into some issues like when I couldn't find the correct ID for the un ordered list items. It always feels frustrating when you can't figure out what you did wrong. Having said that, I am getting faster at identifying the problems. When I was setting up padding for the reflection ID, I couldn't get the change to reflect on the browser. I later found out that I had typed in "Reflections" and not "reflections" in my html. It was a funny and silly reminder to look out for capitalization mistakes. In general I think I'm understanding flexboxes, especially after the mention of them being a different type of box. I always thought there was a reason behind the name, and that's why I was mostly confused by it. Now I know a flexbox allows us to have flexible positioning and space distribution.
